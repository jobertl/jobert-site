## Life Update

I made one of the strangest detours of my life when I went to the Macau Comic Festival on the **30th**, seeing as it wasn’t on my agenda at all until I noticed some promotion for it the previous day. Compared to other similar events I attended in Hong Kong and Vancouver, this was easily the smallest scale, mainly due to Macau’s vastly smaller population. ## Baked Pork Chop Rice Review

This week, I’ll be reviewing the baked pork chop rice from Macau’s Cafe Neptune, which I ate on the **29th**. While I usually order the similar yet uniquely Macanese dish of Atlantic baked rice whenever I’m in Macau, Cafe Neptune unfortunately didn’t have it on their menu.

As a side note, I’ll be reviewing the baked pork chop rice in Hong Kong/Macau according to different standards compared to its Canadian counterparts, mainly due to differences in ingredient quality (Canadian ingredients are higher-quality), cooking technique (Hong Kong/Macau restaurants use pain-fried pork, while Canadian restaurants use cutlet port and fried rice), and differing expectations of portion size (Canadian servings are much larger, but slightly pricier to match).

Sauce: 3/5 - Rather bog-standard, though a bit more sour than usual.

Rice: 3/5 - Good enough, nothing to write home about.

Pork chop: 3/5 - In a departure from the usual Hong Kong/Macau serving style, it came pre-sliced, though its taste and texture were pretty standard.

Additional toppings: 2/5 - With nothing else poking through aside from the basics, the generous helping of molten cheese on top is the only thing saving this category from being a 1/5.

Portion size: 3/5 - Exactly what I’d expect from its price.

Total score and final remarks: 14/25 - Aside from its conspicuous lack of additional toppings (or abundance of cheese), you couldn’t find a more average dish of baked pork chop rice anywhere in Hong Kong or Macau. There’s a very good reason Atlantic baked rice is my go-to version in Macau, though if it’s not available for any particular reason, I certainly won’t say no to eating this again.
