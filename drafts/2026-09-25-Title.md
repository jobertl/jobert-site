## Baked Pork Chop Rice Review

Offered as an in-flight meal on Cathay Pacific. When I saw it on the menu, I knew I had to try it.

Sauce: 3/5 - Mixed well with cheese, and was rather

Rice: 3/5 - I could taste that it had been fried, but it soggy by the time it got to me due to in-flight preparation, similar to the pork chop

Pork chop: 3/5 - Rather small, similar to the rest of the dish. There was an attempt at making it a cutlet, but in-flight preparation resulted in it being soggy

Additional toppings: 4/5 - This was one category that really surprised me, since celery, corn, mushroom, and onions were all included,

Portion size: 2/5 - Way below average size even by Hong Kong standards, and slightly smaller than your average in-flight meal. Still, it was pretty filling, especially when combined with everything else being served.

Total score and final remarks: 15/25 - While I may have been a bit harsh with my scoring in places, especially since I’m comparing something prepared in the sky with its counterpart on the ground, Cathay Pacific’s baked pork chop rice is still my favourite in-flight meal. Despite the logistical and technical difficulties of recreating such a complex dish, Cathay Pacific managed to make something that holds it own against actual restaurants, and I couldn’t be a happier customer. Unfortunately, seeing as I don’t fly with Cathay all that often, it’s likely going to be a long time before I have another chance to taste their bake pork chop rice again.
