## Life Update

I went to Manchester's other Costco on the **4th** after realising that it existed, since it was marked with the suburb it was in rather than as being in Manchester on the map (while the first Costco I went to was also in a suburb, it was marked as Costco Manchester). There, I finally had the chance to try out a British £1.50 hotdog and pop/soda. Even though I thought the beef sausage's meat quality was higher than those I ate in Canada, it lacked the spices found in the Polish sausages I usually had back there. The bun felt more soggy as well, so I'm sorry to say that it doesn't live up to the hype (or maybe I've been spoiled by Canadian Costcos). To make matters worse, all the soft drink options were zero sugar. Boo.

One interesting detail I noticed as I left Costco was that according to a notice on display, only people working in several professions (such as civil servants or lawyers) can become Costco members (at least on paper). This stands in stark contrast to the Canadian model, where anyone with valid ID can freely embrace the glory of Costco membership.

(Addendum: Apparently, the limitation on which professions can become Costco members exist because of some British law restricting what type of people wholesalers such as Costco can sell to, though there are seemingly ways around this.)

There was another studio class on the **5th** where we talked about practice habits and sight-read some exercises, including a few from the Arban book (seeing an oh-so-familiar one on a slide all but flashbanged me). Since I had a practice room reserved for immediately afterwards, I rehearsed some excerpts for an upcoming audition with a couple of other trombones, where we listened to each other and gave feedback.

While I've used the TooGoodToGo app to get food for cheap in Canada before (mainly $8CAD pizzas, Tim Hortons baked goods, and even the occasional Hong Kong-style pastries), my biggest haul with it yet came on the **6th**, courtesy of my local Greggs (a place eerily similar to Tims in niche, but with better food. No, I will not rescind this hot take). Unfortunately, my £3 haul didn't include any sausage rolls, but it more than made up for that with two tomato soups, two six-pack mini pigs-in-a-blankets, two popcorn chickens, chicken wings, mac and cheese, and potato wedges. Talk about a steal!
