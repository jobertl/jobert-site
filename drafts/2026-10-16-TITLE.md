## Life Update

On the non-music side of things, I visited the Manchester IKEA to buy some household essentials, while also getting to enjoy a discounted fish and chips lunch courtesy of the restaurant there. Strangely enough, this was my first time eating fish and chips in Manchester (though not in the UK, given that I've visited London before), and even though it isn't the best rendition of the dish I've ever had, it's certainly got value for money.

Another notable lunch I had was with an old friend of mine who I've known since my days in Hong Kong, where we swapped stories about what we had been up to since we last saw each other. In true English fashion, we enjoyed it at a pub, but the best thing about this locale was that it was on a hill overlooking Manchester. Combined with the excellent weather, quite the rarity in Britain, the sight of sheep and horses grazing in the nearby pastures made this idyllic sight one I'll never forget. (Ignoring the fact that I had a tasty dinner of lamb shank just the night before.)
