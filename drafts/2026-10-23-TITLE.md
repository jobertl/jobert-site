## Life Update

### Music things

Aside from devising exercises for excerpts, the RNCM trombones welcomed several guests from the University of Texas El Paso (UTEP) during this week's studio class, seeing as they were touring around the UK while being based in Leeds for a series of performances. After our regular studio time ended, almost all of us stayed behind to read through some trombone choir pieces with the UTEP trombones (including an arrangement of the 'march' movement from Tchaikovsky 6!), before having some beers at the RNCM bar. Even though I was based in North America up to a couple of months ago, I didn't work with many Texan trombonists during my time there, so this was a pretty good opportunity to get a glimpse into their unique musical culture. Of course, it goes without saying that they were *very* American as Texans, but the enthusiasm they brought to the RNCM was more than enough to smooth over any initial culture shock.

### Non-Music Things

During my trip to Bury Market, I had lunch at the Hongkonger-run 80's Street Food, which consisted of imitation shark fin soup and a battered oyster cake. Strangely, this was my first time finding and having the latter dish outside of Hong Kong, which is doubly weird since Vancouver is also well-known for both its Hong Kong-style food and oysters. Wonder why no one's ever thought of doing that there.
