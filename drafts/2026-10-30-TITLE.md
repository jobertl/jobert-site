## Life Update

### Music Things

This week's studio class was a classic 'play-to-the-class' session with an RNCM twist: we were provided with a college accompanist. While I had a couple other potential pieces in mind for this class, I ultimately decided to play the first movement of Gordon Jacob's trombone concerto, since I believed its mixture of lyrical and technical sections could provide me with the greatest amount of feedback. Even so, this was quite a last-minute change for me, so I had less time to brush up on it than intended, with my resulting performance being noticeably worse than the one at my recital and my Guildhall audition video. Ironically enough, this year's Guildhall auditions was also the reason one of my fellow trombones played this piece for the session as well, and certainly sounded better than my rusty rendition.
