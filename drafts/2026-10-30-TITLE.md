## Life Update

### Music Things

This week's studio class was a classic 'play-to-the-class' session with an RNCM twist: we were provided with a college accompanist. While I had a couple other potential pieces in mind for this class, I ultimately decided to play the first movement of Gordon Jacob's trombone concerto, since I believed its mixture of lyrical and technical sections would provide me with the greatest amount of feedback. This being a last-minute change meant I had less time to brush up on the piece than intended, so my resulting performance was noticeably worse than the one at my recital and my Guildhall audition video, but I did receive some useful pointers on my playing style in the end. Ironically enough, this year's Guildhall auditions were also the reason one of my fellow trombones played this piece for the session as well, and certainly sounded better than my rusty rendition.
